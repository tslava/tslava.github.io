How I use AI

  • Spec first. Interfaces, failure modes and tests are written before agents implement a feature. I review every change.
  • Cross-vendor review. A model from another vendor reviews plans and diffs before merge.
  • Measured, not trusted. Automated evaluations of AI output are checked against human labels before I rely on them.
  • Limited access. Assistants read only what they need; actions that affect external systems stay with a person.
