Case study · At work

The backend behind parking signs that show the right number

Strength
Reliable high-load backends
My role
I own the backend
Stack
Python · AWS Lambda and ECS · PostgreSQL · AWS IoT Core for LoRaWAN · The Things Stack

At the smart parking company where I work, LED signs tell drivers how many spaces are free. I own the backend of this product: it works out occupancy, sends the content to each sign over LoRaWAN and watches that every sign is online and showing the right thing.

What I built

  • One backend, several networks. Signs on different LoRaWAN networks, AWS IoT Core and The Things Stack, are driven by the same backend.
  • Monitoring that tells the truth. Online status and history for every sign, delivery tracking and alerts. Later I moved monitoring into its own service that covers all LoRaWAN controllers.
  • New features. Per-space indicator lights tied to a booking system and manual control of individual sign sections.

Result

Signs can be managed, monitored and diagnosed across networks. Monitoring does not stop every problem, but a sign that shows the wrong thing does not go unnoticed: the team gets an alert and can find the cause.
